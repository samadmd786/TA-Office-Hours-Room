# CLD 101: Intro to Cloud Computing — Syllabus

*Fictional course for a demo. Northfield Institute, Fall 2026.*

## 1. Staff

- Instructor: Prof. R. Alvarez (fictional)
- Teaching assistant: the TA in the `office-hours` room
- AI assistants in office hours: `@triage`, `@helper`, `@insights`
  (see section 6)

## 2. Grading

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Assignments A1–A5 | 50% (10% each) |
| Midterm | 20% |
| Final project | 25% |
| Participation | 5% |

## 3. Late policy

1. Every student has **3 late days** for the term. Use up to 2 on any one
   assignment. You don't need to ask; the submission time is what counts.
2. After your late days are used, a late assignment loses **10% per day**,
   up to 3 days. After 3 days it gets no credit.
3. **Extensions** beyond late days are granted only by the TA or instructor,
   for illness, family emergencies or documented accommodations. Ask in
   office hours or by email **before** the deadline when you can. AI
   assistants cannot grant extensions.
4. The final project cannot use late days.

## 4. Office-hours rules

1. Office hours run in the `office-hours` chat room. Post your question
   there; the AI assistants answer most questions at any hour, and the TA
   is online Tue/Thu 4–6 PM.
2. **One question per message**, and say which lecture or assignment it is
   about (for example "L03" or "A3").
3. For graded work you get **hints, not solutions**. This applies to the TA,
   too. Don't paste your full solution into the room; share the smallest
   snippet that shows the problem.
4. Questions about grades, extensions or regrades go to the TA, not the
   assistants. The assistants will route them.
5. Be kind. Everyone in the room can read every message.

## 5. Academic integrity

You may discuss ideas and debugging approaches with classmates and the
assistants. You may not share or copy solution code or YAML for graded
assignments, or ask anyone (person or AI) to write it for you. Code you
submit must be your own.

## 6. AI assistants in office hours

- `@triage` reads each question, labels it, and sends it to the right place.
- `@helper` explains concepts from the course materials, with citations,
  and gives hints only for graded work.
- `@insights` notices repeated questions and drafts FAQ entries for the TA
  to approve.

The assistants make mistakes. The course materials and the TA are the
authority. Nothing an assistant says changes a grade or a deadline.
